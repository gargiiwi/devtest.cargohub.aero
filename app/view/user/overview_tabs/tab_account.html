Hints for entering a strong password:

- It should contain at least 8 characters
- Both small letters and CAPITAL LETTERS should exist in your password
- Add a few numbers too, preferably not 1234...
- Punctuation like % $ , : ! - greatly increase the strength of the password
- Password can contain spaces [   ]
- For maximum security, unusual characters like |, ~, ] or * which aren't normally used for text are making sure the password is impossible to guess.

Never use your name, birthday, phone number, e-mail or other easily identifiable personal information as your password (or as part of your password)!

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):

{#ifnot:%features.twofa:

You can increase the security of your account greatly by activating Two-Factor authentication.

This feature will require you to use the Google Authenticator on your mobile phone (or other devices) to obtain a security PIN. This number is only valid once and changes value every 30 seconds both on your personal device and on the server.

Even if someone somehow steals your password, without access to your phone or security device (which presumably has it's own passwords or other login methods) they won't be able to access your account, making it nearly-invulnerable to cracking.
When you activate this function you will be required to scan a QR code with the Google Authenticator app, or enter the generated "key" in the app, then type in the PIN which is shown. If it matches, the 2FA will be turned on for your account.

After it is turned on, the only way to turn it off is by contacting support@cargohub.nl
