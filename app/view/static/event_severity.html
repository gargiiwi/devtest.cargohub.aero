Class E - Negligible

Little or no consequences.
"Move along, nothing to see here"

Class D - Minor

Nuisance. Operating limitations. Use of emergency procedures. Minor incident.
"That yellow light is blinking"

Class C - Major

A significant reduction in safety margins, a reduction in the ability of the operators to cope with the adverse operating conditions as a result of increase in workload or as a result of conditions impairing their efficiency. Serious incident and/or injury to people.
"Houston, we have a problem !"

Class B - Hazardous

Large reduction in safety margins, physiucal distress or a workload such that the operators cannot be relied up to perform their tasks accurately or completely. Serious injury and/or major equipment damage.
"Hull breached! Emergency power to shields"

Class A - Catastrophic

Equipment destroyed. Human lives lost.
"All hands abandon ship! Repeat, all hands abandon ..."
