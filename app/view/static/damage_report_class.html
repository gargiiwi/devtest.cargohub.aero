Cargo damage classification

Class 1 - Minor damage

Small tears less than 2 cm each and less than in 3 numbers.
Dens / Scratches occur to outer packing only.

Class 2 - Moderate damage

This type of damage, from visual inspection of the outer packing, can affect the contents.
Inspection of the contents may be necessary.

Class 3 - Major damage

This type of damage clearly affects the condition of the contents.
Inspection and verification is necessary and important for claims purposes.

Class 4 - Possible pilferage / theft

This category is to be used only when the cargo is suspected of pilferage.
This type of damage is deliberte and involves tears and cuts to the outer packing.

Class 5 - Life threatening

Damage involving safety of lives and property.
Inspection, decontamination and verification is necessary e.g. chemical / radioactive spillage and leakages.
