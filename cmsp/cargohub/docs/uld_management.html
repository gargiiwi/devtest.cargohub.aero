AIR CARGO INDUSTRY SOLUTION 

CargoHub offers a web based solution to easily deliver and accept ULDs  between handlers and forwarders. The platform is mobile app supported and eliminates the administrative burden of ULD transfers and stock control which are subjected to inconsitant ULD registrations and the absence of smart technology during ULD transfers.



Our mission is to streamline and standardize ULD care and control process within the air cargo industry and to provide a the cargo industry stakeholders with a digital loss prevention program to prevent financial loss, reduce operations costs and to increase the customer experience and the overall quality of the air cargo producy.

BENEFITS FOR FORWARDERS

If you are dealing with ULD matters from a forwarder perspective you will probably be aware of all the adminstrative burden for managing you ULD stock. 

Excel registration forms, lack of actual stoch overview, damage registration and ULD inquiries from airlines resulting in operational costs and penalties if no proof can be delivered that ULD were actually returned to the airlines handling agent.

The CDM ULD care and control solution hosted by CargoHub provides a simple and effecient solution for forwarders. The standardization of delivery forms at the same increases the speed of ULD data processing  and optimizes data accuracy for ground handling. The platform consequently contributes to an optimized ULD stock control proces for the ultimate beneficairy, the airline. 






BENEFITS FOR HANDLERS 

Any irregularity on cargo shipments negatively affects the services offered to shippers in transportation by air. Many of the irregularities such as missing or damaged ULD are noticed at the handlers acceptance process.

In order to enable airlines to effectively manage their ULD stock control, it is required for handling companies to report missing and/or damaged ULD as quickly as possible to their airline customers.

The ULD care and control program offers a sophistaticated add on for handlers to increase the quality of their services.







ULD control

Handling agents are offered add ons to their operational process to increase accuracy and the speed of ULD stock information to airlines. Full control tools are offered to fully digitize the ULD control process and to increase the quality of the handling services.
