How do I login as a Freemium user?

Go to the CargoClaims homepage and click on the 'Find my Claim' option to login as a Freemium user.



Fill in your credentials to access your personal environment. Press on '[Click here]' or 'Forgot your Password?' if you forgot your password, you will receive an automatic system generated email where you will be able to reset your password. Check your spam box if you cannot find the mail in your inbox.
