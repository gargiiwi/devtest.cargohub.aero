Status and priority

CargoClaims uses a status and priority system to get a better overview of all the files. This way users can expedite the handling of files, sorted on priority and status.

[Click here] to find out more about status and priority within the CargoClaims application.

No response after a claim?

Be sure that you have send out the claim and if you need more information about an individual claims or incidents, please contact the airline.

CargoClaims cannot provide any additional information different from the response you received.

On our carrier page you will find references to most cargo airline websites with contact details where possible.

A useful Global Directory of Airlines and Agency contacts can be found by visiting A-Z Freight.
