How do I register on CargoClaims?

File your first cargo claim to receive a Freemium account by mail. Once you have pressed the send claim button on the communication module of the CargoClaims site you will instantly receive login credentials to your Freemium environment if your email address does not have an account connected to it yet. Please contact us by email if you are interested in our Premium and Enterprise packages.
