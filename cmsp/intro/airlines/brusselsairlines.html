Cargo

Brussels Airlines Cargo is the airfreight department of Brussels Airlines. Using Brussels Airport as a hub, Brussels Airlines Cargo offers you the best cargo services throughout Europe and Africa.

About Brussels Airlines Cargo

Brussels Airlines Cargo is the airfreight division of Brussels Airlines.
Learn more about Brussels Airlines Cargo

Our GSSA's

Brussels Airlines Cargo is working with General Sales and Services Agents to commercialize the belly space of the Brussels Airlines scheduled flights. Our GSSA partner in Europe, the United States and Asia for all cargo activities is Air Logistics Group.
Discover the GSSA's

Products and Services

Brussels Airlines Cargo transports any cargo commodity that may be accepted for transport on passenger aircraft and within the limitations of the holds of these aircraft, except for valuable shipments (Gold, diamonds…).
